**Description:**

Velo PDF is a free, open-source PDF viewer built for huge documents. Open a 10,000-page manual and it's on
screen in about a second; jump to any page instantly, zoom smoothly, and search the whole text. It installs
like a desktop app, works offline, and can be set as your default PDF viewer on Windows. Your files never
leave your device.

**Maker comment:**

Hi Product Hunt! 👋 I made Velo PDF because huge PDFs (manuals, legal bundles, scanned archives) make most
viewers crawl. Velo only reads and renders what's on screen, so size barely matters. It's open source (MIT),
and I'd love to hear which PDFs you throw at it, and what you'd want next: thumbnails? annotations? printing?
